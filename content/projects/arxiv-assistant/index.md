##### Overview

Every day, the bot pulls new papers from arXiv's q-fin and cs.LG categories, summarizes them with an LLM, and posts the digest to Telegram. I built it for myself, to keep up with research on AI innovation and development.

---

##### How it's built

+ Containerized with Docker
+ Developed through pull requests, each reviewed by Gemini
